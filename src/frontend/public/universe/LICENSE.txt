Ship, station, base and satellite models: Ultimate Space Kit by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0. No attribution is required; credit is given with thanks.

The jumpgate (Commercial Jumpgate AX13, CGTrader) is licensed and not redistributed: build it locally with tools/assets/build-jumpgate.mjs; without it gates fall back to shader wormholes.
